# 🌌 MeetGeon-V2

> **A new way to communicate.**

MeetGeon-V2 is an open-source project focused on rethinking online meetings and social interaction. By putting personalization and user freedom first, MeetGeon-V2 empowers everyone to customize their digital environment however they like, proving that giving users full freedom creates a far more expressive and beneficial experience for all.

This repository contains both the official landing page and the early prototype of the project.

---

## 📖 About

The main goal of MeetGeon-V2 is to demonstrate that giving maximum creative freedom to users doesn't bring negative chaos—instead, it unlocks huge benefits for community expression, identity, and engagement. 

Rather than forcing users into rigid, static video grid layouts, MeetGeon-V2 lets everyone tailor their communication space around their own preferences, creativity, and unique identity.

In the future, users will be able to:

- 🎨 Fully tailor their digital meeting rooms and layouts
- 📞 Host simultaneous 4-person video/audio calls across different web browsers
- 👤 Express themselves through custom themes and avatar systems
- 🤝 Visit, share, and interact within unique, user-created spaces
- ✨ Enjoy an environment where no two meeting spaces look or feel the same

**MeetGeon-V2 is built on the belief that ultimate user freedom and deep customization form the foundation of better digital communication.**

---

## 🚀 Vision

MeetGeon-V2 aims to evolve into a fully community-driven, open-source platform.

Our long-term goal is to explore new boundaries in web communication by continuously introducing features shaped directly by user feedback and creative ideas. We want to prove that open, highly customizable software leads to richer online communities.

---

## 🌟 Main Features (Planned)

- 📞 Support for simultaneous 4-person calls across different browsers
- 🌐 Real-time cross-browser peer communication
- 🎨 Deep customization tools for meeting environments
- 👤 Custom avatar and identity expression tools
- 🏠 Personalized digital rooms for small group interactions
- 🔓 100% open-source community edition
- ⚡ Lightweight and accessible web implementation
